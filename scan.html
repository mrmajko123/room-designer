Make a 3D copy of your dresser, desk, bed or anything else. It goes straight to your computer, where you check its size.

3D scan

  1. Scan your item with a free 3D scanner app.
    ScaniverseFree · iPhone & Android PolycamiPhone & Android KIRI EngineiPhone & Android

    Clear space around the item so you can walk all the way round it. Move slowly, keep the whole item on screen and scan in good light. iPhones with LiDAR (Pro models) get the size exactly right.

  2. Export the scan to your phone.

    In the app, tap Share or Export and pick GLB (or OBJ, USDZ, PLY or STL). Save it to Files or Downloads.

  3. Send it to your computer.
