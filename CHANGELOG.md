## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-02

First release of Room Designer: design your room in 3D.

- **Your real room size** in centimetres and metres: rectangle, L-shaped or any shape. Type wall lengths (350 or 3.5m) or drag
  corners in the plan view. Ceiling height, doors, windows and balcony doors
- **Build it from photos**: take a photo of each wall, drag 4 dots onto its corners, and Room Designer
  straightens the photo, measures the wall's length and puts the photo (or just its colour) on your 3D wall
- **Paint and floors**: paint any wall or all of them, brick, panelling, wood slats, stripes; wood,
  herringbone, carpet, tiles, concrete floors, or a photo of your own floor
- **LEDs and lights**: LED strips (solid, breathing, colour cycle, rainbow), an LED strip around the whole
  room, neon signs with your own text, hexagon lights, fairy lights, lamps. Night mode shows them glowing
- **Furniture**: beds, loft bed, desks, gaming and office chairs, sofas, closets (hinged, sliding, mirror,
  walk-in), chests of drawers, nightstands, bookshelves, wall shelves, cube shelves, TV, monitor, gaming PC,
  rugs, plants, posters with your own pictures, mirrors, curtains and more. Every size and colour can change
- **Scan your own stuff with your phone**: in the desktop app, scan a QR code with your phone, then 3D scan
  your dresser, desk or anything else with a free scanner app (or take a few photos). Check its size before
  it goes into My stuff, and it appears in your room
- Open 3D scans directly too: GLB, glTF, OBJ (with textures in a zip), USDZ, PLY and STL
- Drag things around; they snap flat against walls, sit on desks and shelves, and show their distance to the walls
- 3D view (walls in front hide automatically), plan view with measurements, and walk-around view
- Undo and redo, several rooms, save a picture, export a 3D model (.glb), save and open room files
- Works offline; your rooms, photos and scans stay on your device
